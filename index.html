How It Works

Sending a shipment should be straightforward. Follow these simple steps to get your package ready, keep track of its progress and understand the delivery process.

01

Step One

Book Your Shipment

Contact our team with your collection address, destination, package details and preferred shipping option.

02

Step Two

Collection & Processing

Once the booking is confirmed, your shipment can be prepared for collection, handling and dispatch according to the agreed service.

03

Step Three

Track Your Package

Use your shipment tracking reference on our website to check the progress updates available for your package.

04

Step Four

Delivery to Destination

Your shipment proceeds to its destination under the selected delivery arrangement, with updates provided where available.
